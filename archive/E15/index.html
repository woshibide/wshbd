Pyotr Goloub is a computational designer, blurring the line between design and engineering. As design evolves from fixed environments into adaptable systems, he builds his own tooling to reshape the process itself, removing friction between idea and execution. This approach accelerates both his own speed and internal team workflows, without compromising precision or visual quality. Fluent across both creative and technical domains, he understands the underlying mechanics that connect them, allowing him to design with systems, code, and a clear awareness of how implementation shape material and ultimately the outcomes.

Flying Over The Dutch Nest

Book

Netherlands

2024 Nov

Visual essay born out of research into the Best Dutch Design Book archive at Museum Meermano

Flying Over The Dutch Nest takes the Best Dutch Design Book collection as a starting point for looking at how ideas and appearances accumulate around art academies. The publication brings that inquiry into a sequence of visual material, leaving the images room to be encountered without explanatory captions. Its cover includes a rectangular opening, making looking through the book part of handling it. The work gives “eclectic opportunism” a visual setting through collection, framing and sequence, while the relationships between its images remain open to the reader
