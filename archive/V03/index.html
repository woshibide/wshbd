Pyotr Goloub is a computational designer, blurring the line between design and engineering. As design evolves from fixed environments into adaptable systems, he builds his own tooling to reshape the process itself, removing friction between idea and execution. This approach accelerates both his own speed and internal team workflows, without compromising precision or visual quality. Fluent across both creative and technical domains, he understands the underlying mechanics that connect them, allowing him to design with systems, code, and a clear awareness of how implementation shape material and ultimately the outcomes.

Self Initiated

Apparel

WWW

2024 Jul

Happy hour. May your coffee be sweet. Forward momentum, clarity of purpose. Good luck in business endeavours and skill in decision-making. Strategic moves, optimised processes

I combined two visual cultures on one shirt: the office worker’s collared uniform and imagery associated with Russian prisoners’ tattoos. Blue ballpoint-style drawings cover the pale blue fabric, turning its familiar working-day form into the setting for another set of signs. The work follows the kind of visual pairing I associate with Vladimir Tsesler, where bringing two things together can produce a third meaning. The resulting garment carries that collision on the body, with the shirt and its marks remaining recognisable as different sources
