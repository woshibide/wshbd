Pyotr Goloub is a computational designer, blurring the line between design and engineering. As design evolves from fixed environments into adaptable systems, he builds his own tooling to reshape the process itself, removing friction between idea and execution. This approach accelerates both his own speed and internal team workflows, without compromising precision or visual quality. Fluent across both creative and technical domains, he understands the underlying mechanics that connect them, allowing him to design with systems, code, and a clear awareness of how implementation shape material and ultimately the outcomes.

Rock, Paper, Symbols

Publication

Netherlands

2024 Jul

A periodical that brings critical writing into a layered editorial object

Rock, Paper, Symbols gives critical writing a dense reading format through narrow columns of text. Its exposed stitching also makes the construction of the publication visible as an object. The typography holds sustained passages together across the spreads, while the binding shows how those pages are gathered. The work joins a concentrated reading surface with a physical structure that can be inspected. That relationship gives the periodical a material presence alongside the arguments it contains
