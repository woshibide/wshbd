Pyotr Goloub is a computational designer, blurring the line between design and engineering. As design evolves from fixed environments into adaptable systems, he builds his own tooling to reshape the process itself, removing friction between idea and execution. This approach accelerates both his own speed and internal team workflows, without compromising precision or visual quality. Fluent across both creative and technical domains, he understands the underlying mechanics that connect them, allowing him to design with systems, code, and a clear awareness of how implementation shape material and ultimately the outcomes.

A self-initiated magazine that brings feminist publishing history into the form of an erotic publication, pairing an oral-history conversation with photographs

I Am My Lover is a self-initiated magazine built around a conversation with Joani Blank and Leigh Davidson about publishing, feminism and sexual liberation. Their account provides a historical context for making and circulating material on their own terms. The magazine places that conversation alongside photographic material, bringing the history into a contemporary editorial object. The work asks how an erotic publication can also carry an account of who gets to publish and represent sexuality. Its form keeps the historical voices present within the reading experience
