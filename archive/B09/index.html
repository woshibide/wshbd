Pyotr Goloub is a computational designer, blurring the line between design and engineering. As design evolves from fixed environments into adaptable systems, he builds his own tooling to reshape the process itself, removing friction between idea and execution. This approach accelerates both his own speed and internal team workflows, without compromising precision or visual quality. Fluent across both creative and technical domains, he understands the underlying mechanics that connect them, allowing him to design with systems, code, and a clear awareness of how implementation shape material and ultimately the outcomes.

Montré

Visual Identity

Russia

2023 Jul

A quiet identity for a furniture reseller with rustic French character

Montré’s furniture and accessories provided the starting point for an identity centred on rustic French references. Earthy colours and vintage elements carry that character into the shop’s visual language. A restrained serif wordmark gives the name a quiet presence, including on pale printed material. The identity connects the objects being sold with the atmosphere around them, drawing on associations of warmth and craft. It gives the shop a consistent way to present its furniture and accessories through type, colour and material
